Plan Your Visit

Come and spend an exciting day at San Diego Zoo with you family and friends.

What to do

You'll need to book a flight to the San Diego International Airport, and buy tickets, either online (Lower Prices but more wait) or here (Higher Prices but no wait).

What to bring

  • A Hat
  • Sunglasses
  • Phone / Camera
  • Comfortable shoes

A Recommended Plan:

  1. Come to San Diego
  2. Get a taxi
  3. Arrive here
  4. Buy tickets
  5. Wait for friends or family (Optional)
  6. Visit the Lions from a range
  7. Meet the Polar Bears
  8. Feed the Gorillas
  9. Have a Break
  10. See the Koalas
  11. Look at the Hippos
  12. Watch the Red Pandas
  13. Visit more animals (or go back home)
  14. Take some photographs to share online (Optional)

Opening Hours

Monday - Thursday: 9:00AM - 6:00PM

Friday - Sunday: 9:00AM - 9:00PM

Location:

11 minutes drive from the San Diego International Airport.

2920 Zoo Dr, San Diego, CA 92101, United States
